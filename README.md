Your Guide to Medical College Admissions in India

MedEdfill aims to help NEET aspirants explore medical college options based on their NEET scores and admission eligibility.

🌐 Live Website: https://vikramsuthar0909-bit.github.io/MedEdfill/

🎯 About MedEdfill

Choosing the right medical college can be challenging. MedEdfill is designed to make the college-search process easier for students exploring medical courses.

🎓 Courses Covered

- MBBS – Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery
- BDS – Bachelor of Dental Surgery
- BAMS – Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery
- BHMS – Bachelor of Homeopathic Medicine and Surgery

⭐ Project Goals

- Explore medical colleges.
- Understand NEET-based admission options.
- Explore course-wise college eligibility.
- Make medical admission information easier to access.
- Initially focus on Gujarat, with an aim to expand across India.

👨‍🎓 Who Can Use MedEdfill?

- NEET UG aspirants
- Medical admission applicants
- Students exploring MBBS, BDS, BAMS and BHMS
- Students comparing college options

⚠️ Important Disclaimer

MedEdfill is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with NTA, MCC, AACCC or any government counselling authority.

College eligibility, cutoffs, seat availability and admission rules may change every year. Always verify information through official counselling authorities and college websites.

A predicted eligibility result does not guarantee admission.
